As a User
I want the app to open quickly from a cold start, ideally in under a second
so that opening it to listen or check for new episodes never feels like waiting

Note: under a second is a target ("would be nice"), not a hard limit. It is measured from tapping the icon to a usable screen. See [[app-startup]] for what is and isn't measured.
