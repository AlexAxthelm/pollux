# Mini player
---
priority: important
---

When ever an episode is playing (or active, but paused), and we're not in the
player view, there should be a
miniplayer visible, so that essential info and actions are available at a glance
/ touch.

UI:
* small bar, full width
* play/pause button
* Episode title, feed title
    * Click here (or swipe) expands to full player view
* from: source/playlist
* Progress indicator as thin strip: Not a touch target

Classy touch: When transitioning from the full player to the mini (or back), a
transtion showing that the previous view is contained in the mini player would
be nice. Same for starting a new episode (episode row expands into player as an
"open" action indicator)
