# Screenshot bookmarks
---
priority: low
---

The user can allow the app to process screenshots that they have taken, which
include player information, and if the app can identify the episode and timestamp, it creates a bookmark at the timestamp in the screenshot.

This falls out of a habit that I've found myself getting into where I'm listening to a podcast and there's some point that I want to come back to in that episode later when I'm not on my walk or driving in my car. And what I have been doing is: with my screen locked and just the default UI media player view there, I take a screenshot. And in that screenshot with my current application (Overcast), I get the episode artwork, I get the title, the feed name, and I get the timestamp out of how long. So like I've got one here: it's "noisia radio S03E41" from feed "Vision Radio", timestamp 6:46 (-53:14 remaining).

In general, that seems like sufficient information to be able to drop a bookmark into an episode, or at the very least bring up that point of the episode again.

This very much will tie into the bookmarking feature.
